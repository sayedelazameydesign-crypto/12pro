# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in AGI-OS a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards

- Be respectful, inclusive, constructive
- Focus on 12-layer architecture quality
- Provide evidence (gates, benchmarks) not just claims
- Respect governance and security policies

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported via GitHub Issues with `code-of-conduct` label or direct to maintainers.
